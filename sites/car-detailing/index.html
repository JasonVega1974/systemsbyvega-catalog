Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Do I need to be home?+
Nope. As long as we can reach the car and you've left it unlocked (or dropped a key), you can be at work the whole time. We'll text you before and after. Most folks come out to a finished car.
Do you bring your own water and power?+
Yes — we're fully self-contained. Our own water tank and power come with the van, so we can detail in a driveway, a parking lot, or a job site. If a spigot or outlet is handy we'll happily use it, but we never count on it.
How long does it take?+
Depends on the package and how the car's been living. An Express Refresh is about an hour; a Full Detail runs a few; a Showroom Detail is a half-day. We'll give you a real window when you book — not a vague "sometime today."
What if the car's really rough?+
Send a couple of photos when you book — pet hair, spilled latte, constructions-site dust, we've seen it. If it needs an add-on or a bump to the next package, we'll tell you the number up front. No surprise line items when we're done.
How do I pay?+
Card, tap, or the usual apps — whatever's easy, paid when the work's done and you've looked the car over. The price you booked is the price you pay, plus any add-ons you okayed.
Where do you go?+
Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Kuna, Star, Garden City and everything in between — the whole Treasure Valley. Just outside the ring? Text us your address; if the route works we'll make it work.
