A standing appointment, not a favor you have to chase

Most people do not want a cleaning service so much as they want one evening a week back. So we run this like a standing appointment: a set weekday, a set crew, a checklist your household wrote, and a price that was settled at the walkthrough instead of at the door. If something is not right, you tell us and it is on the list from then on — that is the whole arrangement.

Nell Prescott · Yarrow & Broom

How it works

Four steps, then it runs itself

Walk the house

We come see the place first, room by room, and ask what actually bothers you. The walkthrough is free and there is nothing to sign at the end of it.

Get a flat price

You get the per-visit number in writing before anything starts — set by the rooms and the cadence you pick, never by how long the day happens to run.

We clean, you carry on

Same crew, same weekday. Leave a key or a door code; most households are at work while we work and walk into it finished.

Tell us what to shift

After a visit we ask what to do more of. Your checklist changes that day and stays changed, so the fourth visit fits better than the first.

      Questions

      The things people ask first

      How long does a visit take?+
      A standard clean on a three-bedroom house usually runs a morning with two cleaners. The price does not move with the clock, though — you agreed to a flat number before we started, and that is the number.
      Do I need to be home?+
      No. Most clients leave a key, a lockbox or a door code and come home to it done. If you would rather be there the first time, that is completely normal and we will book it that way.
