A season, not an afternoon

Most people who hang their own lights do it once, in the cold, and then live with whatever went up. We measure the rooflines in the fall, cut the runs to fit your house, and store them labelled so the second year goes up in a morning. When a run goes dark in the middle of December, that is ours to come out and fix, not yours.

How it works

Book in the fall

We walk the house, measure the rooflines and put the design and the figure in writing. Nothing is ordered until you say yes.

Install in November

Runs are cut to your measurements, clipped to the shingle line or gutter edge, and set on a timer before we leave.

Serviced through December

A bulb out, a run down after a windstorm, a timer drifting — call and we come out. It is already in the price.

Down in January

We pull everything by mid-January, coil it labelled by roofline, and store it until next season.

What a season includes

Commercial-grade bulbs

C9 and C7 lamps on heavy lead wire — the same stock that goes up downtown, not the strands that turn brittle at ten below.

Cut to your house

Every run is measured and cut for one roofline, so there is no slack looped over the gutter and no dark gap at the peak.

Clips, never staples

Shingle and gutter clips carry the wire. Nothing is stapled, nailed or screwed into your roof.

Timers set on site

A photocell or programmed timer goes in at install, so the display comes on at dusk without anyone touching a switch.

Service calls included

Outages, wind damage and timer drift are covered from install through takedown. No trip charge in season.

Takedown and storage

Removed by mid-January and stored labelled by roofline, so a second season is an install and not a redesign.

      Questions

      When should I book?+
      Installs run from the first week of November until the week before Christmas. Booking in September or October gets you a date you actually want.
      Whose lights are they?+
      Ours. The price is a seasonal package rather than a purchase — we supply, install, maintain, remove and store the material. If you would rather own the lights outright, ask and we will quote that separately.
      What happens if a section goes dark?+
      Call us and we come out. In-season service is part of the package, so there is no trip charge between install and takedown.
      Do you put holes in the roof?+
      No. Everything hangs on shingle tabs and gutter clips. Nothing is stapled, nailed or screwed into the roof or the fascia.
      What if it snows on install day?+
      We work through normal winter weather but not on iced roofs or in high wind — that is a safety call, not a preference. If we push a date we tell you the same day and rebook you at the front of the line.
