Privacy Policy

This explains what Grit & Gleam Powerwashing does with your information when you contact us or book a job.

1. Who holds your information

Grit & Gleam Powerwashing is responsible for the information you give us. We operate in Nampa, ID, and you can reach us on (208) 555-0151 or at hello@gritgleampowerwash.com.

Our website is built and hosted by Systems by Vega. They provide the software; they are not the ones you are hiring, and they do not decide what happens to your information.

2. What we collect

  • What you type into the form on this site — usually your name, phone number, email address, the job you want done, and where it is.
  • What you tell us directly — by phone, text, email, or in person while we are arranging or doing the work.
  • Job records — what we did, when, and what it cost, so we have a record of our own work and can answer a question about it later.

We do not run advertising trackers on this site, and we do not build a profile of you.

3. What we use it for

To reply to you, quote the job, turn up at the right address, do the work, invoice it, and deal with anything that comes up afterwards. If you ask us not to contact you again, we will not.

4. Who else sees it

Your enquiry is delivered to us by email through the site's hosting and email providers, and it reaches us the same way any email does. We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not hand it to anyone for their own marketing.

We may have to share information where the law requires it, or with someone helping us complete your job — a supplier, or a specialist we bring in. Anyone in that position gets only what they need for that job.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet our tax and record-keeping obligations, and then we get rid of them. If you want us to delete what we hold about you sooner, ask — and see section 6.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at hello@gritgleampowerwash.com and we will deal with it. Some records we may have to keep for a period even after you ask — an invoice, for instance — and we will tell you if that applies.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under your state or national privacy law. Those rights apply whether or not they are listed here.

7. Cookies

This site uses only what it needs to work and to remember your choice about this notice. It does not carry advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Children

This site is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top.
