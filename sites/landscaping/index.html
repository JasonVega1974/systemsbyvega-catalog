Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Do I need to be home for a visit?+
No. Gate code or a note on where things are is all we need — most of our regulars never see us show up. We text before and after, so you always know it's done.
What happens if it rains — or snows — on my scheduled day?+
We reschedule to the next clear day, usually within 24-48 hours, and text you the new time. No visit gets skipped without a replacement — you're never charged for one we didn't do.
Do you bring your own equipment?+
Yes — mowers, edgers, blowers, aerators, the works. You never need to own or store anything for us to do the job.
Can I switch plans, or skip a visit?+
Anytime. Text us before your next scheduled visit and we'll adjust the plan or push the date — no cancellation fee, no long-term contract holding you in.
What's not included in a plan?+
Bigger jobs like irrigation repair, tree removal, sod or hardscape work aren't part of a standard plan — see the "What's outside a standard visit" list above. Text us a photo and we'll quote those separately.
Where do you service?+
Meridian, Boise, Eagle, Star, Kuna, Middleton and everything in between — the whole Treasure Valley. Outside that ring? Text us your address anyway; if a route works we'll make it work.
