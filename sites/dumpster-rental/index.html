Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Will it wreck my driveway?+
No. Every delivery includes wood boards under the rails so the box never touches your concrete, and our drivers set it down easy — no dragging. We've placed hundreds of boxes on stamped and decorative driveways without a mark.
How much weight is included?+
2 tons (4,000 lbs) is baked into every price. Most household projects never touch the limit. If you're tossing heavy stuff — concrete, dirt, tile — overage is a flat $65 per ton, billed only if the landfill scale ticket shows it. We forward you the ticket, so you never take our word for it.
What if I need it longer than 14 days?+
Easy — it's $10 per extra day, no re-booking, no re-delivery fee. Just text us before your pickup date and we'll push it out. Long remodels do this all the time.
What if I fill it early?+
Text us and we'll grab it — usually next business day. Early pickup never costs extra. Need to keep going? We'll swap the full box for an empty one and only the second rental gets billed.
Do I need a permit?+
On your own driveway or property — no. On the street, most Treasure Valley cities want a simple right-of-way permit (usually $25–$50 and same-day online). Tell us where it's going when you book and we'll point you at exactly the form your city wants.
Where do you deliver?+
Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Eagle, Caldwell, Star, Kuna and everything in between — the whole Treasure Valley. Outside that ring? Text us your address anyway; if a route works we'll make it work.
