Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Do you subcontract the work out?+
Almost never. Our own crew does concrete, carpentry, hardscape and finish work. The exceptions are licensed-trade tasks like electrical and plumbing rough-in on remodels — and if we bring in a licensed sub for those, we tell you up front and we manage them, not you.
How do estimates work?+
Free, on-site, and in writing. We walk the project with you, take measurements, and usually have a written quote to you within 48 hours. The price on that paper is the price — it only changes if you change the scope, and we re-quote in writing before continuing.
How long will my project take?+
Punch-lists and pressure washing: usually one visit. Driveways, decks and fences: one to three weeks including cure time. Kitchen and bath remodels: we build a milestone schedule into your quote so you know what happens each week. We'd rather quote you a real timeline than a flattering one.
Do you take small jobs?+
Yes — gladly. Punch-lists are a service line, not a favor. Small jobs are how most of our long-term customers found us, and they get the same written quote and the same cleanup as a full remodel.
What area do you serve?+
Nampa is home base, and we work the whole Treasure Valley: Caldwell, Meridian, Kuna, Middleton, Star and Boise's west side. Outside that ring? Text your address — if it's a bigger project we'll often still make the drive.
