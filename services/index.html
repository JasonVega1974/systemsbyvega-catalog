How I work

Same six steps, every time.

No sales team, no project manager, no black box. One person does the work, in the same order, on every build — and the tools I use to do it are part of why the price and the timeline are what they are.

Step 1

Brief

You describe the problem in plain language — what's broken, and what a fix has to do.

Step 2

Spec

That becomes a written specification — exact screens, fields, and rules — so nothing gets guessed once building starts.

Step 3

Plan

The spec breaks into a step-by-step build plan, in pieces small enough that each one can be checked on its own.

Step 4

Build

I write and run the code myself. Much of it goes through AI coding agents that I direct and review line by line — the same process that built EstateSaleBiz, GarageSaleBiz, and ConsignmentBiz — which is why one person can do in weeks what used to take a small team.

Step 5

Review

Every piece gets checked against the spec before it ships — not after you find the bug.

Step 6

Ship

You get a working system running on your real data, not a demo, and you own it at the end.
