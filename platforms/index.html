Platform 01 · Sale & Resale

EstateSaleBiz

EstateSaleBiz is the operating system for running a full estate sale: pricing tools for the contents of a house, staffing and day-of checklists, and a public storefront where buyers browse what's for sale before the doors open. Each city on the platform gets its own checkout and buyer notifications, built once and reused by whoever holds it.

It's for someone willing to run the sale itself — walking a house, pricing the contents, staffing the day — not someone looking for leads to resell.

Open estatesalebiz.com →

Platform 02 · Sale & Resale

GarageSaleBiz

GarageSaleBiz turns a single garage sale into a business: a public map of every sale across your three cities, a listing tool sellers use themselves, and the Saturday-morning traffic that comes from being the map people actually check before they get in the car.

It's for someone who wants to own the map — the directory a town opens on sale day — not run one individual sale for one individual family.

Open garagesalebiz.com →

Platform 04 · Sale & Resale

ConsignmentBiz

ConsignmentBiz runs the storefront and paperwork behind a consignment shop: intake, splits, and a public site where buyers browse what's currently on the floor. The software handles the bookkeeping a shop like this normally runs on a shared spreadsheet.

It's for someone taking in other people's furniture and finds on a split, without carrying the inventory or the storefront lease themselves.

Open consignmentbiz.com →
