A house full of things is a house full of decisions

Most families call us in one of three seasons: a passing, a move, or a long-overdue downsize. Whichever brought you here, the job is the same — treat the household with respect, research and price things honestly, and leave you with an empty, swept house and an itemized accounting of everything that sold. You keep what matters. We handle the rest.

How it works

From first walkthrough to swept floors

Walk & assess

We tour the house with you, room by room, and flag anything the family wants to keep. The walkthrough is free, and there's no pressure to book.

Stage & price

Our crew sorts, stages and tags every room — tables up, cabinets opened, and each item researched and priced against real estate-sale comparables.

Sale weekend

We run the sale from open to close: signage, crowd flow, checkout, and half-price hours at the end. You don't need to be there — many families prefer not to be.

Broom-clean handoff

Whatever remains is donated or hauled away by arrangement. Then we hand back a swept, empty house along with an itemized settlement sheet.
