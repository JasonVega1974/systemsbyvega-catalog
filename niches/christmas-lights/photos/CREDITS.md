## Considered and rejected

A Victorian turret outlined in warm bulbs (Dmitry Spravko, unsplash.com/photos/gPpmLi8uJmM)
was initially shortlisted and then rejected on inspection: the hanging baskets are in full
summer bloom and the trees in full green leaf, so it is a restaurant's year-round decorative
lighting photographed in summer rather than a holiday install. A readable "...dray Restaurant"
sign in the same frame was the lesser problem.

No before/after pair ships with this niche. Stock does not carry the undecorated "before" of a
lit house; the closest candidates were two different buildings, and the lit one showed cafe
string lights on a porch rather than a roofline install. The slider was dropped rather than
captioned around, so the site makes no same-house claim it cannot support.
