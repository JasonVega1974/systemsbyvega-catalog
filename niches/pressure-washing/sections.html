Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Will this damage my paint or siding?+
Not with our technique. Anything painted, stuccoed or vinyl-sided gets soft-washed — low pressure, a cleaner with real dwell time, and a gentle rinse. Full pressure is reserved for concrete, pavers and brick, which can take it.
How long does a driveway take?+
Most single driveways run 45–90 minutes start to finish, depending on how bad the staining is. Full exterior washes are usually a half-day.
Do I need to be home?+
Nope. Point us to an outside hose bib (and an outlet if the job needs one) and we'll text you before/after photos when it's done.
What if it's raining or about to freeze?+
We reschedule at no cost — pressure washing in a hard freeze just refreezes the water on the surface, which helps nobody. We'll text you a new time as soon as the forecast clears.
Where do you clean?+
Pressure washing vs. soft washing — what's the difference?+
Pressure washing uses mechanical force (water pressure) to blast dirt off hard, tough surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses lower pressure plus a cleaning solution with dwell time to lift algae, mildew and grime off surfaces that pressure alone would damage — paint, stucco, shingles. Most jobs use both, matched to the surface.
