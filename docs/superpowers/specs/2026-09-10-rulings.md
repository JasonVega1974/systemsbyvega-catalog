Twenty-two decisions taken on Jason's behalf while executing the plan, each with what it
costs if it turns out wrong. Preserved here because the working ledger they were recorded
in is scratch and does not survive.

## The one thing deliberately left for Jason

`/sites/` still carries the retired done-for-you story — *"I white-label it"*, *"your domain
connected, hosting set up"* — and a second Web3Forms enquiry form promising a reply in one
business day, one scroll from self-serve Stripe checkout. `/` now describes the real
current flow. The two pages disagree about what $299 buys.

That copy is pre-existing; the reorganization is only what put the two stories next to each
other. It is a statement about what a customer receives, so it is not an agent's to
rewrite. See the final review's finding 7.

## Superseded

**R7 and R14 — 2026-09-11.** The hero no longer runs the two-frame
demo-then-branded sequence. Jason asked for a rotation through the six featured
niches (bin-cleaning, landscaping, dumpster-rental, dog-walking, dj, child-care),
one every ~5s, cross-faded and looping, each frame that niche's own hero captured
from its built site.

What carried forward from the rulings it replaces:

- **R7's rule still holds** — every frame is a screenshot of something actually
  running. The six are the `featured` REST frames from `tools/build-shots.js`,
  captured from `/sites/<niche>/`, not hand-made and not a logged-out `/admin/`.
- **R14's lesson still holds** — a frame must not be captioned in a way its own
  pixels contradict. The old frame 2 read *"Your site."* while wearing a for-sale
  banner. The rotator sidesteps this: frames carry the **trade** only
  ("Bin cleaning"), so the demo banner each screenshot legitimately wears agrees
  with its caption instead of arguing with it.
- **Trades, not brand names.** The demo brands are examples, qualified by a
  disclaimer that sits with the cards further down the page. Repeating them in the
  hero, above that disclaimer, would read as a client roster.

Cost if wrong: a hero that shows breadth but no longer narrates the rebuild-under-
your-brand story. That story still exists in full on `/services/` and in How-it-works.
`assets/shots/hero/2-branded.jpg` survives as the `og:image` and is the only
remaining consumer of the `hero` shot group.
